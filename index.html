rclone backup

A private tool that backs up its owner's own files from their Mac to their own Google Drive, using rclone. It is not offered to, or usable by, anyone else.

Privacy policy

  • Who uses it: only the owner of this site, with their own Google account.
  • What it accesses: only files and folders that the tool itself created in the owner's Google Drive (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other Drive content.
  • What it stores: the backed-up files, in the owner's own Google Drive. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Sharing: no data is collected, sold, or shared with anyone, and nothing is sent to any server other than Google Drive.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
